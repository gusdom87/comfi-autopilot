Comfi Autopilot

An autonomous operations system for short-term rentals, connected to Hospitable. Start with the first step of the operation: paste a guest message. Triage returns a category, a confidence, a draft in the operator's voice, a route, and a reason, plus the send right that category has earned so far. The urgency decision itself is made by Jev, an existing decision tool (TypeSafe AI) the operator runs inside Comfi Autopilot.

Plays: the operation at work

Staged demo world. The properties, turnovers, cleaners, and arrivals below are fixture data — no real guest data anywhere. Every send in a play is simulated or held for approval: nothing real is sent and nothing real is charged. Action steps show the gate the autonomy ladder applies before anything may execute.

Play: early check-in

A guest asks to arrive early. Comfi Autopilot extracts the requested time, checks the staged calendar (is there a same-day turnover? who cleans? when is the unit actually ready?), drafts the reply, and gates the early check-in charge — the highest-stakes action — for a human. When the answer depends on the cleaner, the run waits, and the cleaner's report finishes the chain.


Delivers the cleaner_done event to the waiting run.

Play: cleaner done → guest ready

No guest message at all: the cleaner finishing is the trigger. Comfi Autopilot verifies the clean against the record, then the arriving guest hears the place is ready. If the cleaner flagged a problem, the guest hears nothing and a human takes over.

Play: camera check

An outside camera reports motion — no message from anyone. Comfi Autopilot classifies the staged frame, checks it against the staged calendar, and either logs it (the guest who was expected, the cleaner who was scheduled, nothing of note) or flags the operator (a crowd bigger than the booking, a person nobody expected, a frame too dark to judge). This play never messages a guest. The frames are staged metadata, not real photos.
